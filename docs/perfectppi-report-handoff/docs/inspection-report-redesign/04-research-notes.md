# PPI inspection workflow and tire research

Researched 2026-09-22. Primary sources only. These findings inform the requested plan; they are not implementation or a finished report template. Recommendations below are our product-design inferences, not claims that competitors implement them identically.

**Decision update:** This document preserves the initial research and provisional recommendations. The user's subsequent answers and the [developer handoff](05-developer-handoff.md) supersede them wherever they differ. In particular, every confirmed puncture or embedded foreign object requires replacement under the chosen PerfectPPI program policy, even when technical repairability may be possible. Tread is required with an explicit unavailable exception for self-inspectors, and selecting **Include photo evidence appendix** creates a separate PDF with all findings and uploaded photos and no page limit. The initial questions below have been resolved in the [updated plan](01-discovery-and-plan.md).

## Proven patterns to borrow

- **Tekmetric:** mobile inspections attach photos/videos/PDFs directly to an inspection task, offer preset findings plus editable notes, distinguish checked/okay, attention soon, immediate attention, and not applicable, preserve unselected tasks as incomplete, show completion progress, and promote urgent findings for review. Adapt task-linked evidence, reusable finding choices, and an exception review queue. Do not import its “rate all” shortcut as an automatic pass for unseen items. [Performing an Inspection](https://support.tekmetric.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037472154-Performing-an-Inspection)
- **Tekmetric:** circles, arrows, and text notes make photos more understandable in reports. Offer a simple concern marker and short note, with original image retained. [DVI Image Markup](https://support.tekmetric.com/hc/en-us/articles/34327534094871-DVI-Image-Markup)
- **AutoVitals:** a capture checklist includes vehicle corners, VIN/door-jamb identity, running instrument cluster/mileage, all four treads, sidewall specifications, and pertinent evidence of findings. Its advisor review checks image relevance, completeness, and quality and can return an inspection for correction. Adapt this into guided framing and a pre-submit review that requests a retake only for the problematic view. The source’s shop checklist is not a requirement to add inaccessible technical checks to a consumer flow. [Photo Best Practices](https://support.autovitals.com/hc/en-us/articles/8754627477012-AutoVitals-DVI-Photo-Best-Practices)
- **Hunter Quick Tread Edge:** purpose-built scanning measures edge-to-edge tread and builds a visual result, with up to six groove measurements per tire. This supports recording inner/center/outer measurements to expose uneven wear. It does not establish that an ordinary phone photograph yields trustworthy millimeters. [Hunter brochure](https://www.hunter.com/globalassets/hunter/products/vehicle-inspection/unmanned-inspection/documents/insp-qcd-wlpr-brochure-7632te.pdf)
- **UVeye:** its dedicated tire/wheel scanner markets DOT/specification extraction and visible wheel/tire issue detection, with categorized reports. Borrow the idea of one capture session supporting several structured fields and grouped evidence. Its results rely on specialized hardware; do not transfer its claimed detection/measurement capability to this app’s phone workflow without validation. [Wheel and tire workflow](https://uveye.com/wheelrestoration/), [hardware overview](https://www.uveye.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/UVeye_Automotive_Brochure_4_DIGITAL-Version_LR-2.pdf)

## Tire facts and corrections to the raw notes

1. **Load and speed are different fields.** Load index is a number, such as `91`; speed rating is the letter, such as `V`. Together `91V` is the service description. XL is a separate extra-load designation. Preserve the full original sidewall string plus parsed size, load index, speed symbol, and XL/load range; allow unknown rather than guessing. [Michelin ratings](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tires-101/tire-load-rating-speed-rating)
2. **DOT age and pressure:** the last four TIN digits are week/year; `0224` is week 2 of 2024. A complete date may be on only one sidewall. Calculate age as of inspection date, not report download date. Cold pressure is measured after at least three hours without driving; record the actual measurement and use the vehicle placard/manual target, not tire-sidewall maximum. NHTSA also allows another size recommended by the vehicle manufacturer; exact sticker mismatch is a discrepancy to review, not universal proof of unsafe fitment. [NHTSA TireWise](https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/tires)
3. **Fitment exceptions:** approved alternate sizes and manufacturer-specified front/rear differences exist. Compare LF/RF against the front specification and LR/RR against rear; preserve evidence supporting a documented alternate fitment. A missing/unreadable placard is “unable to verify,” not “mismatch.” [Michelin performance upgrades](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tire-buying-guide/performance-upgrade)
4. **Tread units:** 2/32 inch is approximately 1.6 mm; Michelin recommends replacement at the wear indicators. Store a canonical measured value and convert for display; 1/32 inch = 0.79375 mm exactly. Avoid labelling every other threshold legal or universal. For a user-selected mm or 32nds entry, preserve original unit and rounding precision. [Michelin replacement guidance](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tire-buying-guide/when-do-i-need-new-tires)
5. **Cracking:** “dry rot” can be shown as visible cracking/weathering. Fine superficial cracking differs from deep/extensive cracking; confirmed outward bulges differ from harmless inward construction indentations. A four-color rubric is a product policy to validate with a tire professional, not a recognized standardized age-to-color formula. Age alone cannot establish cracking severity. Severe cracking on a recent tire should trigger confirmation of the photo/date and professional assessment, not an invented cause. [Michelin sidewall guidance](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tire-damage/sidewall-problems)
6. **Urgent damage, age, ZR, wear causes:** USTMA lists exposed cord/fabric, bulges, deep cuts/cracks, and damaged wheels among conditions requiring removal from service. Service life varies with storage, usage and environment; chronological age cannot predict it alone. Z in ZR is a high-speed size designation, with R radial construction; use the actual service-description speed symbol for rated capability. Uneven wear can prompt inflation/alignment/suspension evaluation but does not prove a failed suspension component. [USTMA Tire Care and Safety Guide, printed pp. 8, 19–20, 24–25](https://www.ustires.org/system/files/files/2024-05/Tire%20Care%20and%20Safety%20Guide%2002%2022%2019%20HR.pdf)
7. **Foreign objects and repairability:** an embedded object plus pressure loss needs urgent professional inspection, not an unconditional “replace” rule. Holding pressure also does not prove repairability or safety. Repair eligibility depends on location in the repairable tread area, puncture size (at most 1/4 inch/6 mm), remaining condition and internal inspection after demounting. Sidewall damage and a confirmed bulge require replacement. Professional combined internal patch and puncture-channel fill is distinct from an external plug. A photograph cannot establish internal damage or sustained pressure retention. [Michelin repair criteria](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tire-maintenance/can-my-tire-be-repaired)

## Proposed easier capture flow

1. Choose assisted self-inspection or technician inspection; ask available tools once. A self-inspector can photograph visible evidence and enter gauge measurements if equipped. Mark inaccessible/unmeasured checks explicitly. Technician mode can accept verified measurements, lift/underbody work, tests, and diagnostic attachments.
2. Capture identity, odometer/dashboard, and placard once. Autofill extracted text, then show a compact editable confirmation. Ask for VIN/date/size clarification only when needed.
3. Use a consistent clockwise walkaround with a visible vehicle diagram and named positions. Capture context per corner, then targeted close-ups for sidewall text/DOT, tread/gauge, rim or defect. Let one image support several fields with evidence references; do not require duplicate uploads for each question.
4. For each tire show an editable card: position; brand/model; size/service description; DOT; tread; actual and target pressure; cracking; wear; tire damage; wheel condition; fitment. Offer “not visible,” “not measured,” and “not checked,” independently of green/yellow/orange/red. Never default those states to green.
5. Record inner/center/outer tread where tools allow; otherwise show “not measured.” Photo-based visual wear observations and numeric gauge measurements must be distinct. Pressure includes cold/warm/unknown and source. “Holding pressure” requires an observed test/history, not a single photo or one pressure reading.
6. Progressive questions: an object reveals location, pressure-loss observation and service recommendation; a suspected bulge requests a side-angle view and confirmation; uneven wear asks for a pattern and relevant symptoms; fitment discrepancy shows installed versus expected values and alternate-spec evidence.
7. Before submission show only missing essentials, low-confidence extraction and conflicts, plus all critical findings. Offer per-field edit/retake and save/resume. Finish with the requested accuracy attestation; store its exact wording/version, signer identity, timestamp, and inspection version. Any later inspection edit invalidates the old attestation.

## Two-page report implications and decisions needed

- Page 1 is a fixed geometry visual summary with status symbols/text, a tire grid, damage locations and scope/completeness. Provide “not inspected” separately from not applicable; compact packages must not look like full mechanical checks were performed.
- Page 2 can use “Condition Overview” with grouped findings, urgency, evidence, implications and next actions. Avoid claims beyond the input evidence, unsupported causes, or silent omission of critical items to fit the page.
- PDF should include report/inspection ID, version and date; full-resolution photos can remain in the existing secure digital report, referenced by stable IDs/link or QR if approved. A two-page hard cap requires explicit overflow rules; it cannot be solved by shrinking text indefinitely.
- Clarify the inspection audience, whether four-level color applies throughout or just cracking, mandatory measurements/tools, what “complete” includes, whether advisory fitment mismatches differ from confirmed unsafe load/size/rubbing conditions, PDF paper size, and whether photos/details may be linked outside the two pages.
