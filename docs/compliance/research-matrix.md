# Federal, State, and Platform Research Matrix

Research date/access date: September 2, 2026. Status: engineering research for licensed-counsel validation, not legal advice. Threshold conclusions require actual PerfectPPI user/household/revenue/sale-share metrics.

Implemented nationwide baseline: notice, access/export/correction/deletion/opt-out/appeal intake, authorized-agent email path, nondiscrimination statement, and GPC recognition. Operational verification, deadlines, sensitive-data consent, processor contracts, data-protection assessments and recipient lists remain open.

No comprehensive consumer privacy law was identified as in force in the sources reviewed for the jurisdictions below as of the research date. This does **not** mean no privacy law applies: all have breach/security or sectoral rules, and website, biometrics, minors, communications, vehicle, repair, insurance/VSC and other laws may apply.

## State-specialty workstreams

- Biometrics/face/voice: Illinois BIPA and other state biometric laws require a feature-specific review before identification, templates or face geometry are processed.
- Washington My Health My Data and similar consumer-health statutes require review if vehicle/inspection/support information is used to infer health status; the current product should not do so.
- California CalOPPA requires a conspicuous website privacy policy when its trigger is met; the public policy and update date are implemented.
- Every state’s breach law, repair/inspection law, VSC/insurance code, auto-renewal law, mini-TCPA and UETA/e-sign rules require specialist counsel before the relevant feature or incident.
