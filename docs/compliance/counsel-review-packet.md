# Counsel Review Packet

Prepared September 2, 2026. Engineering status: implemented pending licensed-counsel approval.

## Product summary

PerfectPPI is a U.S.-intended web and iOS platform for guided vehicle inspections, OBD context, AI-assisted reports, marketplace/community/messages/reviews, technician and organization workflows, partner delivery, and currently blocked vehicle-service-contract workflows. Required public wording: “PerfectPPI is a product managed by DnD Solutions & Optimization LLC.”

## Documents for review

- Public version 1.0 pages: Privacy Policy, Notice at Collection, Privacy Choices, Terms, Community Guidelines, AI Disclosure, Accessibility, Copyright, Support and Service Contract Disclosure.
- Engineering evidence: `data-inventory.md`, `vendor-and-data-flow-map.md`, `research-matrix.md`, `gap-analysis.md`, `retention-schedule.md`, `provider-compliance.md`, WISP and incident response.
- Blocking facts and decisions: `open-questions.md`.

The public Terms intentionally contain no governing-law choice, arbitration/class waiver, indemnity, warranty disclaimer or liability cap. Payment/refund/cancellation and VSC terms are not invented. Counsel should return a redline and identify which changes require renewed clickwrap.

## Priority opinions requested

1. Entity/contact facts, enforceable Terms, age/eligibility and assent design.
2. Inspection agreement/state addenda, repair/inspection licensing, safety/roadworthiness language and technician/organization classification.
3. Marketplace role, seller/dealer disclosures, tax/payments and review/moderation risk.
4. VSC/warranty role and a state-by-state launch matrix covering provider/obligor/administrator/producer, licenses, forms, financial security, cancellation/refunds, claims, taxes and compensation.
5. Privacy-law threshold/applicability analysis using actual business metrics; minors, biometrics, location, VIN/DPPA, media and AI.
6. Category retention, legal holds, deletion/processor propagation and evidence preservation.
7. DMCA agent/notice/counter-notice/repeat-infringer program and illegal-content escalation.
8. E-SIGN/UETA consent, contract delivery and retention.
9. Marketing channels, CAN-SPAM postal address, TCPA/mini-TCPA and suppression.
10. Dispute, limitation, insurance and indemnity strategy.
